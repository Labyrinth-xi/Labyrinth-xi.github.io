## Research Profile

My research interest is in electrochemical materials and how data can make experimental decisions more reliable. In my current work, I study carbon based multi metal catalysts for electrocatalytic reactions, with a focus on methanol oxidation and formate production. I connect catalyst composition, electrochemical response, product quantification, and model feedback so that each batch of experiments can guide the next one.

## Research Experience

**Bayesian Optimization and High Throughput Screening of Carbon Based Electrocatalysts**  
Nanyang Technological University | Aug 2025 to Present

- Prepare carbon supported multi metal catalysts and evaluate their electrochemical performance in clean energy related reactions.
- Build experimental datasets that record catalyst composition, loading, preparation conditions, electrochemical activity, product yield, and selectivity.
- Use Python controlled pulse heating to tune nanoparticle loading and dispersion on carbon cloth, which supports faster sample preparation and batch comparison.
- Carry out electrochemical measurements in a three electrode cell with an ion exchange membrane. The main tests include cyclic voltammetry and chronoamperometry.
- Quantify formate produced from methanol oxidation electrolytes using NMR and ion chromatography.
- Combine CA charge, average current, formate concentration, yield, and Faradaic efficiency to compare catalyst performance.
- Write Python scripts for electrochemical data processing, NMR and IC calibration, concentration calculation, and FE summary generation.
- Analyze Bayesian optimization results together with experimental observations. I check model error, batch effects, candidate ranking, and local refinement before planning the next compositions.

**Composition Design and Electrochemical Performance of Carbon Nanofiber Based Composite Electrodes**  
Undergraduate Thesis, Zhengzhou University | Nov 2023 to Jun 2024

- Designed and prepared carbon nanofiber based composite electrodes with active components such as NiCo2S4 and MoS2.
- Used hydrothermal growth and doping modification to improve electrochemical energy storage behavior.
- Improved charge transfer and electrode reaction kinetics through Ni doping. The best sample reached a specific capacitance of 995 F g-1.
- Used CV, GCD, and EIS to study charge transfer resistance, ion diffusion behavior, and the influence of composition design on electrochemical performance.
- Thesis grade: 90/100.
