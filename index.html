Research Focus

Data-driven electrochemical materials research

Electrocatalysis

My current work focuses on methanol oxidation reaction (MOR) systems for formate production, with catalyst compositions spanning Ni-rich multi-metal and carbon-supported high-entropy alloy materials.

High-throughput workflows

I combine rapid catalyst preparation, CV and CA testing, and structured experimental databases to shorten the cycle from synthesis to performance evaluation.

Product quantification

I use NMR and IC to quantify formate, then integrate electrochemical charge, yield, average current, and Faradaic efficiency to evaluate catalytic activity and selectivity.

Bayesian optimization

I analyze active-learning model performance, batch effects, local refinement, and candidate selection strategies to guide the next experiments in composition space.
