Research Focus

Electrochemical materials, measured carefully and improved iteratively

I am interested in how catalyst composition changes reaction behavior. In my current project, each sample is not just a current curve. It becomes a data point with composition, preparation conditions, charge, product yield, and model feedback. This lets me compare materials more clearly and decide what to test next.

01

MOR electrocatalysis

I study methanol oxidation systems for formate production, especially Ni rich and multi metal catalyst compositions supported on carbon based substrates.

02

Product analysis

NMR and IC are used to quantify formate. I then connect the product amount with CA charge, average current, yield, and Faradaic efficiency.

03

Experiment flow

I use fast sample preparation, electrochemical testing, and Python scripts to reduce manual processing and keep each batch comparable.

04

Model guided design

Bayesian optimization helps me choose new compositions. I also check model error, batch effects, and local refinement before trusting a recommendation.

Selected Projects

Research projects

2025 - Present | Nanyang Technological University

Bayesian Optimization and High Throughput Screening of Carbon Based Electrocatalysts

My current project combines catalyst synthesis, electrochemical testing, product quantification, and active learning. I prepare carbon supported multi metal catalysts, test their MOR performance, and process the resulting data with Python. The goal is to make catalyst screening more systematic, not just faster.

For each batch, I compare composition, CA integrated charge, average current, formate concentration, yield, and Faradaic efficiency. When the model suggests new candidates, I check whether the suggestion makes chemical sense and whether earlier high performing points can be reproduced.

  • MOR
  • Bayesian optimization
  • High throughput testing
  • NMR and IC
  • Faradaic efficiency
2023 - 2024 | Undergraduate Thesis

Composition Design of Carbon Nanofiber Based Composite Electrodes

I designed and prepared carbon nanofiber composite electrodes containing active components such as NiCo2S4 and MoS2. Through Ni doping, the electrodes showed improved charge transfer and reaction kinetics. The best material reached a specific capacitance of 995 F g-1.

This project trained me to connect synthesis choices with electrochemical behavior. I used CV, GCD, and EIS to study charge transfer resistance, ion diffusion, and energy storage performance.

  • Carbon nanofibers
  • NiCo2S4
  • MoS2
  • CV, GCD, EIS
